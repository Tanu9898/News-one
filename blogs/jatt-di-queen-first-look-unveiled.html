Jatt Di Queen: First Look Unveiled as Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions Announce 2027 Release

The upcoming Punjabi film Jatt Di Queen has officially entered the spotlight with the unveiling of its first-look poster. Presented by Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions, the movie is scheduled to release theatrically on 12 February 2027.

The poster announcement received an encouraging response from audiences, prompting the makers to thank fans and supporters who liked, shared and commented on the reveal.

Prabh Goraya and Vicky Kamboj Produce Jatt Di Queen

Jatt Di Queen is produced by Prabh Goraya and Vicky Kamboj, with Arun Kumar Barnwal directing the film and Sumit Bhatt serving as Writer.

The film has assembled a large cast featuring King B Chouhan, Rumman Ahmad, Malwai Partap, Sammy Gill, Honey Mattu, Neha Dayal, Pahulpreet Singh, Lucky Kalsi, Deush Mehra and Sukhdev Barnala.

Extensive Crew Working Behind the Film

The filmmaking team includes several professionals across production, cinematography, editing and technical departments.

Ajay Maurya is the Executive Producer, while Harnek Singh and Rah Sharma are credited for Cinematography. Rohit Dhiman serves as Editor.

The title song has been edited by Gurjeet Singh, with Monika Rani as Title Song Choreographer. Deush Mehra is also credited as Choreographer.

The Assistant Director is Jasdip Singh, while Harshdeep Singh is the Art Director. Vishal is the Camera Operator and Jaskaran Chogawan is credited as DIT.

The technical crew further includes Sharandeep Singh as Gaffer Assistant, Manpreet Singh as Focus Puller and Kuldeep Singh as Gaffer.

Music and Songs of Jatt Di Queen

The film has an extensive music lineup, with Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy serving as Music Directors.

The lyricists include Happy Raikoti, Youngveer, Amber Vashisht, Abeer and Sufraaz. Malwai Partap is credited as Playback Singer, while Manoj Gadarpuriya and Sachin Dahar are among the singers associated with the project.

Post-Production and Promotional Team

The post-production work for Jatt Di Queen is being handled by Rabab Studio, while AME Digital has been announced as the Digital Partner.

The promotional material has also involved K Raj Famous Films for Poster Stills and Manoj Zore for Poster Design.

With so many professionals working across different departments, the film brings together a broad creative and technical team for its 2027 theatrical release.

Jatt Di Queen Release Date: February 12, 2027

The makers have announced 12 February 2027 as the theatrical release date for Jatt Di Queen. The movie is being positioned as a Valentine's Day cinema release, inviting audiences to celebrate the occasion with Punjabi cinema.

The release of the first-look poster is only the beginning of the promotional journey. With the film's cast, crew and music team now revealed, audiences can expect further updates as Jatt Di Queen moves closer to its February 2027 theatrical release.

For Punjabi cinema followers, Jatt Di Queen is now one of the upcoming Punjabi movies to watch ahead of Valentine's Day 2027.
