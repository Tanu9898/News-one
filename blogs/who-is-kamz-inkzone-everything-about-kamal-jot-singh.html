Who Is Kamz Inkzone? Everything About Kamaljit Singh's Music Career, Songs, Collaborations and Inkboy Musik

Kamz Inkzone, the professional name of Kamaljit Singh, has developed a multi-faceted career in the Punjabi music industry. Over the years, he has worked as an artist, music creator, songwriter, DJ and music video producer.

His journey, which began in 2017, has taken him from his first song “Ink You” to collaborations with artists such as Ninja and Bohemia, international projects in London, Dubai and Europe, and the establishment of his own company, Inkboy Musik, in 2026.

Kamz Inkzone's Early Music Career

Kamaljit Singh started his professional music journey in 2017 using the artist name Kamz Inkzone.

His first track was “Ink You.” During the early stage of his career, he also invested in a big-budget music video in Dubai, gaining valuable experience in music video production.

This early exposure helped him understand that creating a successful music project involves much more than recording a song.

Learning Music Production at Speed Records

Kamz Inkzone later worked with Speed Records, where he gained hands-on experience in multiple areas of music production.

He learned about music editing, lyrics, videos and the overall process of creating music.

This experience became an important foundation for his later work, allowing him to independently handle different parts of music and video production.

Befikra With Ninja in 2019

In 2019, Kamz Inkzone worked with Punjabi singer Ninja on “Befikra.”

The track became a major milestone associated with his rap music journey and helped him gain wider recognition within the Punjabi music industry.

After this period, more singers began approaching him for music projects.

Launching Hunar Sidhu and Sade Siro

Kamz Inkzone also became involved in introducing and developing new artists.

One of the significant projects was Hunar Sidhu's “Sade Siro.” The track crossed 25 million views, bringing considerable attention to the project and Kamz Inkzone's contribution to the music-making process.

The success further encouraged him to work with new singers and explore different forms of Punjabi music.

Kamz Inkzone and Bohemia

A major collaboration in his career came when Kamz Inkzone featured with Bohemia.

The collaboration became an important moment in his journey and connected his work with the wider Punjabi rap landscape.

International Shows and Music Videos

Kamz Inkzone has also worked internationally, travelling to London, Dubai and Europe for music shows, private performances and music video projects.

His international work has given him opportunities to experience different audiences and expand his creative network beyond the Indian music industry.

Becoming a DJ and Exploring Remix Music

Kamz Inkzone later entered the DJ space and learned DJing. He started making DJ songs and experimenting with different sounds.

In 2025, he began creating Afro music remixes and Punjabi remixes, while also working on his own original music with featured artists.

His work gradually expanded into songwriting as well, with Kamz Inkzone beginning to write his own lyrics.

Inspiration From Punjabi Literature

Apart from music, Kamz Inkzone has developed an interest in Punjabi literature.

The writings of Shiv Kumar Batalvi and Bulleh Shah have been among the literary influences he has explored while developing his own approach to songwriting.

This has contributed to his transition from simply working on music to creating his own lyrics, music and music videos.

Kamz Inkzone's Work With Speed Records, Taj Music and Stylebase

Throughout his career, Kamz Inkzone has worked as an artist with organisations and platforms including Speed Records, Taj Music and Stylebase.

These experiences have contributed to his understanding of different aspects of the music and entertainment industry.

Inkboy Musik: The Next Chapter

In 2026, Kamz Inkzone established his own production company, Inkboy Musik.

The company focuses on music and music video production and brings together artists from different genres.

Its catalogue includes Qawwali, hip-hop, DJ remix music and commercial Punjabi songs, reflecting Kamz Inkzone's interest in working beyond a single musical style.

Kamz Inkzone's Journey Continues

From releasing his first track “Ink You” in 2017 to working with Ninja, Bohemia and emerging artists such as Hunar Sidhu, Kamz Inkzone's career has developed across multiple areas of the music industry.

Today, his work combines music production, songwriting, DJ music, remix creation, artist collaborations and music video production through his own platform, Inkboy Musik.

With a growing catalogue and a multi-genre approach, Kamz Inkzone continues to build his identity both as an artist and as a creator working behind the scenes in Punjabi music.
