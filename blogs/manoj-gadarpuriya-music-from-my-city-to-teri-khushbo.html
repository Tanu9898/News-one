From "My City" to "Teri Khushbo," Manoj Gadarpuriya Shows a Different Side of His Music

An artist's musical identity is often revealed through the variety of work they create. While one song may introduce listeners to an artist, a collection of different releases shows the bigger picture.

For Manoj Gadarpuriya, that picture is becoming increasingly diverse.

His recent catalogue moves between solo music, collaborations, energetic tracks and more emotional projects. From “My City” and “KUDI FIRE” to the recent romantic song “Teri Khushbo,” his releases demonstrate a willingness to explore different moods and creative directions.

Starting With “My City”

One of the notable solo releases in Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent catalogue is “My City.”

Released on July 7, 2026, the song runs for 2 minutes and 11 seconds and is credited directly to Manoj Gadarpuriya.

A solo release like “My City” gives listeners a direct introduction to an artist's own musical presentation.

Rather than depending on a featured artist, the track places Manoj Gadarpuriya at the centre of the project.

A More Energetic Direction With “KUDI FIRE”

The mood changes with “KUDI FIRE,” a collaboration between Manoj Gadarpuriya and Malwai Partap.

The song has a runtime of 3 minutes and 8 seconds and is credited to Manoj Gadarpuriya featuring Malwai Partap.

The track adds an energetic dimension to his catalogue and demonstrates how Manoj Gadarpuriya can approach music differently depending on the concept and collaborators involved.

It also forms part of his ongoing musical association with Malwai Partap.

“My State My Pride” Brings Identity Into the Music

Another collaboration from the same period is “My State My Pride.”

Released on July 7, 2026, the song is credited to Manoj Gadarpuriya featuring Malwai Partap and has a runtime of 2 minutes and 46 seconds.

The project adds another layer to Manoj Gadarpuriya's catalogue, showing that his music can also explore themes connected with identity and pride.

Alongside “My City” and “KUDI FIRE,” it contributes to a varied run of releases.

“Teri Khushbo” Takes a Romantic Turn

The latest creative direction is noticeably different with “Teri Khushbo.”

Presented as a romantic song, the project moves away from the energetic identity of tracks such as “KUDI FIRE” and into a softer, more emotional space.

The release features Harry Balachour, with the artwork also crediting Ajay Maurya as executive producer and Gurjeet Singh for poster and editing.

The visual presentation of “Teri Khushbo” reinforces its romantic character, creating a cinematic atmosphere around the song.

For Manoj Gadarpuriya, the release is another example of his willingness to experiment with the mood and presentation of his music.

The Harry Balachour Connection

“Teri Khushbo” also continues Manoj Gadarpuriya's musical association with Harry Balachour.

The two previously worked together on “Adat Ban Gai,” which is officially credited to Manoj Gadarpuriya featuring Harry Balachour.

The newer romantic release gives their collaboration a different character.

Instead of simply repeating the same formula, the two projects demonstrate how the same creative partnership can be used for different musical concepts.

Even Darker Themes Appear in His Work

Manoj Gadarpuriya's music also includes the project “Dark Night,” which takes a more atmospheric and introspective approach.

The artwork describes it as “a song for our generation” and credits Manoj Gadarpuriya for singing and lyrics, with Harry Balachour handling music and the feature.

The darker visual identity and theme provide another contrast to the romantic presentation of “Teri Khushbo.”

This difference between projects is what makes his growing catalogue interesting to follow.

A Catalogue That Doesn't Follow One Formula

One thing becomes clear when looking across Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent releases: there isn't one single formula defining his music.

  • “My City” represents his solo side.
  • “KUDI FIRE” brings an energetic collaborative approach.
  • “My State My Pride” adds a strong identity-driven theme.
  • “Dark Night” explores a darker atmosphere.
  • And “Teri Khushbo” brings romance and emotion into the picture.

Each release contributes another piece to the overall Manoj Gadarpuriya identity.

Why This Variety Matters

For an independent artist, variety can be valuable because it gives listeners more than one reason to follow the music.

Someone might discover Manoj Gadarpuriya through an energetic collaboration such as “KUDI FIRE” and later find a completely different side of his work through “Teri Khushbo.”

That journey from one song to another can help turn an individual release into a broader audience connection.

A Musical Identity Still Taking Shape

Manoj Gadarpuriya's catalogue is continuing to develop, and that may be its most interesting characteristic.

Rather than presenting a fixed musical identity, his recent projects suggest an artist still exploring what he can do with different sounds, themes and collaborators.

As his catalogue grows, these individual releases will collectively define the sound and identity associated with his name.

Looking Ahead

With several releases and collaborations already adding variety to his catalogue, the next phase of Manoj Gadarpuriya's music will be worth following.

The response to his newer romantic work, particularly “Teri Khushbo,” adds another direction to his recent output, while songs such as “My City,” “KUDI FIRE” and “My State My Pride” show the broader range of projects already associated with his name.

For listeners discovering Manoj Gadarpuriya songs today, there is already a growing catalogue to explore.

And if his recent run of releases is any indication, there is likely to be plenty more music ahead.
